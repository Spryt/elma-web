Elasto Mania — browser port

Play Elasto Mania online, free, right in your browser. Elasto Mania (also known as Elma) is the classic 2D motorcycle trial game created by Balazs Rozsa and released in 2000. This fan-made web port faithfully recreates the original physics engine and lets you ride the original 54 Internal levels plus 700+ community levels — OLP, Lost Internals, ALP, Elma Online level pack, ElmaPack and Abula — with no installation and no download.

Features: original Elasto Mania physics, ghost riders (your personal best and world-record ghosts, both optional), world-record replays of the classic internal levels, per-level best times with WR reference times for every map, alternative level packs, dimmed brick/stone backgrounds on wall levels such as 29 Headbanger and 40 Double Trouble, and a death-rewind (3 tries, +5 s penalty) that helps most on long runs.

Controls: arrow up gas, arrow down brake, arrow left/right volt, Alt turn, R restart, Esc pause, Tab level select.

Original source code: github.com/elastomania/elma-classic. History of the game (1994–2018): the history of Elasto Mania.
